**Posture:** WCT is a **JFA instance whose economy-education shape bends** — a physical-sport coordination app, not a credit marketplace. The shape may bend; the floors do not: WCT is under the covenant from birth because it puts members in physical play with strangers, and its missing harm track is a **named interim on a committed path**, not an optional feature (OPEN-QUESTIONS #2). It carries one declared covenant boundary:

**The Elo boundary (declared):** the ranked rating is a game-skill score, never trust. It gates ranked matchmaking only; it never appears as reputation-of-conduct and never feeds moderation, credit, or governance standing. If that boundary breaks, COV-1 breaks with it (OPEN-QUESTIONS #1).
